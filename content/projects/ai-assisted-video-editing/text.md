# AI-Assisted Video Editing Workflow

![Codex and ChatCut AI-assisted editing cover](./public/cover.jpg)

## A production experiment

I tested whether conversational AI could remove repetitive editing work without removing editorial control. The workflow connects a reusable, semantically tagged media library to a written story structure, then uses Codex and ChatCut to select footage and generate a timeline that remains fully editable.

## From footage to an editable first cut

![Four-stage AI-assisted editing pipeline](./public/pipeline.jpg)

### 01 — Understand the footage

Source videos are reviewed and tagged by brand, look, shot type, angle and action. The labels stay in a reusable media library instead of disappearing after one edit.

### 02 — Define the story

I write the scene order, pacing and selection rules before generation. AI receives an editorial brief rather than an unstructured folder of clips.

### 03 — Generate an editable timeline

ChatCut uses the brief and semantic tags to select source ranges, order clips and build the first cut. The result is a real multi-track timeline—not a flattened, unchangeable video.

### 04 — Review and refine

I remain the director and reviewer. Changes are requested through conversation, then music, captions and visual labels are added only after the structure is approved.

## The working system

![Codex direction beside ChatCut media, preview and editable timeline](./public/editable-timeline.jpg)

The interface keeps direction, source media, preview and timeline visible together. This makes every AI decision reviewable and lets the editor continue from the generated structure instead of starting over.

## Evidence from a real release

The 30.6-second Gym Outfits edit combined 25 shots selected from six source videos. Manually reviewing, identifying, selecting, ordering and labelling the same footage would take me an estimated two to three hours; that is a personal workload estimate, not a platform benchmark.

The AI-edited output was published as normal social content, and the explanatory workflow video later reached 118K views, 4,712 likes and 3,170 saves on Douyin in the 26 August 2026 account snapshot.

## Human direction, AI execution

This is not autonomous editing. I define the story and constraints, review every cut and decide what is publishable. AI handles repeated source interpretation, selection and timeline operations so human attention can stay on narrative, taste and final quality.
