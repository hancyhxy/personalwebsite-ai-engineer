## Two projects, one showcase

At Tech Fest | AI Showcase, I presented two working prototypes that move from interaction design into implementation: Linkaroo, a runnable iOS product, and Anonymous Connection, a web-and-hardware social installation.

## Software Engineer Showcase

**Linkaroo** is a runnable SwiftUI prototype that helps newcomers to Australia find context-aware community information and one-to-one volunteer support. I used an AI-assisted, spec-driven workflow to move from product concept and interface system to nine connected product screens.

[View Linkaroo on GitHub](https://github.com/hancyhxy/linkaroo)

![Linkaroo iOS prototype](./public/linkaroo.jpg)

## Physical Computing Showcase

**Anonymous Connection** explores a lower-pressure way for strangers to meet. Participants create an avatar from their current mood and interests; a browser experience, collective wall and ESP32 screens carry that identity into a shared physical interaction.

[Try the live experience](https://hancyhxy.github.io/anonymous-connection/) · [View on GitHub](https://github.com/hancyhxy/anonymous-connection)

![Anonymous Connection collective avatar wall](./public/anonymous-connection.jpg)
